# Decision: art identity and features from the outside review (2026-09-19)

An outside review rated the book's niche uniqueness about 7/10: art
direction 7, individual illustrations 5, the complete presentation 8. Its
main finding was style drift: Creation reads as cut paper, while Daniel,
Christmas and the storm move toward smooth generic 3D. It recommended
consistency over the "as realistic as possible" direction, and listed
ToonyStory features worth adapting.

## Decided by the owner ("approved")

- Keep the richness and detail; retire "as realistic as possible"; pin the
  paper-cutout look with a written style bible and a mechanical check on
  prompts (`design/style-bible.md`, `gen.py`, `check_prompts.py`).
- Order of new work: style bible, loved-one narration, print pages, name
  personalisation, Life of Jesus stories 3 to 5, then export package,
  Spanish and gift editions after the pilot and the distribution decision.
- Not adopted: selectable art styles, AI-written retellings without human
  review, child photos, the child inside a Bible scene.

## Why

The product's uniqueness is the whole interactive book. A single richer,
more realistic picture makes the book less distinctive, not more. Loved-one
narration fits every constraint the app already has (no network, no
account, nothing leaves the device) and is the hardest feature for a free
Bible app to copy.
