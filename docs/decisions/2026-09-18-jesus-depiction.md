# Decision: how Jesus is drawn

- **Date:** 2026-09-18
- **Decided by:** the owner ("1 default")
- **Decision:** Jesus is drawn face on, warm and expressive, in the book's
  style, as every other person is. No halo, no averted or hidden face, no
  denominational iconography. Concretely: a man in his early thirties, warm
  brown skin, dark shoulder-length hair and a short beard, a cream tunic
  with a simple blue-and-cream striped mantle, sandals, kind steady eyes.
- **Why it matters:** some traditions prefer not to depict his face; the
  spec flagged the choice before the first adult sheet existed. The
  character sheet `design/characters/jesus.png` is the consistency anchor
  for stories 2 to 5; changing the decision later means regenerating every
  Jesus layer.
- **Applies to:** Jesus calms the storm, the Good Samaritan, Easter,
  Pentecost. The Christmas story shows him as a baby only.
