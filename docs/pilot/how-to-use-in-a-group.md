# Bible Adventures in a group: one page for the room

**Set-up.** Tablet held sideways on a stand or mirrored to a screen. The
picture keeps its full shape either way. Sound on if the room is small;
off and read it yourself if it echoes. Grown-up settings are behind ⚙ on
the library screen; children do not need them.

**A session, about fifteen minutes.**

1. **Pick the story** together from the library. Five stories: Creation,
   Noah, David, Jonah, Daniel.
2. **Read a page.** Little level is short sentences for pre-readers; Big
   level is the fuller story with a verse reference. Press ▶ to have the
   tablet read the page aloud, or read it yourself. Arrow keys or the
   Next button turn pages.
3. **Find things.** Each picture has two or three spots to tap; a hunt
   prompt under the picture says what to look for. Let a child tap. The
   first find of each spot earns a sticker that the whole group can see in
   the sticker book (★ on the library screen).
4. **Quiz.** After the last page, Finish opens two or three questions. A
   wrong tap greys out with a kind nudge; nobody fails. Stars are for
   first-time-right answers.
5. **Family time.** After the quiz: one question to talk about, a short
   prayer to say together, the memory verse, and one thing to try this
   week. Read it from the tablet or press the speaker to have it read.
   This is the part meant for you to lead, not for the tablet.

**Between groups.** Progress (stickers, quiz stars) is on the tablet, not
in an account. To start clean for the next group: Settings → Clear
progress. This does not touch the pilot usage count.

**If something goes wrong.** Reload the page. If the picture holds still,
Calm mode is on (Settings), or the tablet has reduced motion switched on.
No internet is needed after install; if a page is blank, the install did
not finish caching: open the app once with internet.

**Please do not** copy the pictures, print pages, or share the link beyond
your group during the pilot.
