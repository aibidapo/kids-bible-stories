# Church and homeschool pilot

Four weeks, two or three groups, the current five stories. The aim is to
learn what a group actually uses and asks for before anything institutional
is built (roadmap phase 5). Free access. The art is generated and its
licensing stance is still being decided (roadmap phase 2a), so during the
pilot the app is for use in your sessions only: please do not copy pictures
out of it, print them, or share the link beyond your group.

## What the leader gets

- The app link, installed to a tablet's home screen (Share → Add to Home
  Screen on iPad; the install banner or menu → Install app on Android).
  Once installed it works with no internet.
- `how-to-use-in-a-group.md`: one page for the room.
- `feedback-form.md`: to fill in after weeks 2 and 4.

## Before the first session (leader, five minutes)

1. Install the app on each tablet you will use and open one story so it
   finishes caching.
2. Open the grown-up settings (⚙ on the library screen) and switch on
   **Group pilot → Keep a usage count on this device**. Read what it says:
   it counts pages, taps, quizzes and Family time cards per day on that
   tablet, keeps no names or times, and sends nothing anywhere.
3. Choose the reading level for your age group under **Reading level**.
4. Decide whether sound and read-aloud should be on in your room.

## During the pilot

- Use the app however fits your session; do not change how you teach to
  suit it. What you skip is as useful to know as what you use.
- Note anything a child said or did that surprised you, that day, in one
  line. The feedback form has space.

## What to send back

- After week 2 and after week 4: the feedback form.
- After week 4: the usage count from each tablet. Open Settings → Group
  pilot → **Copy log**, paste it into an email. Then **Clear log**, which
  also switches the count off.
- One observed session, if you are willing: the owner sits in the room and
  watches, saying nothing. The leader script in
  `docs/discovery/interview-kit.md` is what they will ask afterwards.

## What happens with it

The counts and forms go into a written findings note
(`docs/decisions/`) that reorders the roadmap: whether the Life of Jesus
stories, printables, leader guides or something else comes next. Nothing
from the pilot is published.
