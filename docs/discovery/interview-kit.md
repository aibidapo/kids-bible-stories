# Phase 0 discovery: interview kit

Roadmap phase 0 (`docs/roadmap.md`). Ten parents of 3–12 year olds, three
church children's-work or homeschool leaders. Twenty to thirty minutes
each, in person or on a call, with the app installed on a phone you hand
over. No code, no feature promises. Write each interview up the same day
in `docs/discovery/interviews/<date>-<role>-<n>.md` using the template at
the end; the findings note goes in `docs/decisions/` when all are done.

## Ground rules

- Ask, then stay quiet. Silence gets the real answer.
- Never explain the app before the watch-them-use-it part.
- Do not defend anything. Write down the criticism word for word.
- "Would you pay?" is a bad question; "what did you last pay for?" is a
  good one. Ask about the past, not the future.
- Same questions to everyone, so answers can be compared.

## Parent script

**Today**

1. What does bedtime, or faith time, look like in your house at the moment?
2. What do you use for Bible stories with your child now? (Books, apps,
   YouTube, church, nothing.) How did you find it?
3. What did that cost you? When did you last pay for anything like it?
4. What is annoying or missing about what you use?

**Watch (hand over the phone, say nothing)**

5. "Open a story and read it with me as you would at home." Note: which
   story, whether they tap pictures, whether they switch Little/Big, how
   they react to the quiz, whether they notice Family time, where they
   stall. Time it.
6. After: "What was that like?" Then: "What would your child do here that
   you didn't?"

**Value**

7. If this disappeared tomorrow, what would you use instead?
8. Of these, which one would you want first, and why: more stories, stories
   about Jesus, the Family time card, printable colouring and stickers,
   a real narrator's voice, a profile per child? Pick one, then a second.
9. You said you paid `X` for `Y`. Where would this sit next to that: less,
   the same, more, or wouldn't pay?
10. Who else in your circle would want this? Could I talk to them?

## Leader script (church, homeschool)

1. What curriculum or materials do you use now, and what does it cost the
   church or the family per year?
2. How is a session run: group size, ages, devices in the room, projector
   or tablets, printed handouts?
3. What do the volunteers or parents struggle with most?
4. Watch: run one story on a tablet as you would in a session, then the
   quiz. Note what they say about size, sound, control of the room.
5. What would a group need that a family does not? (Leader notes, a
   printable, a way to reset between groups, a bigger picture?)
6. What is the approval path for new material in your setting, and who
   decides?
7. Would you run a four-week pilot with free access? What would you need
   from me to do it?

## Findings note (after all interviews)

One page in `docs/decisions/<date>-discovery-findings.md`:

- Buyer segments actually seen (not hoped for) and what each pays today.
- Top three requested items with counts.
- Price points heard, per segment.
- What stalled people in the watch step, with counts.
- Reordered phases 4–7 with one line of reasoning each.
