# Family time content: review pass (2026-09-18)

Self-review by the author of the content, against the story text and the
scripture reference of each story. This is not the human review the roadmap
asks for; it is the preparation for it. Section 3 is the checklist for that
review.

## 1. Findings that need an owner decision

- **Memory verses are NIV wording.** All five (Genesis 1:1, Genesis 9:13,
  1 Samuel 17:45, Jonah 2:2, Daniel 6:22) match the New International
  Version word for word. The NIV is copyrighted (Biblica). Its standard
  permission allows short quotations without written consent when the
  translation is identified and the notice is shown, but the app currently
  shows no translation name and no notice anywhere. Options: (a) add the
  NIV notice to Settings and next to each verse; (b) switch the verses to a
  public-domain translation (World English Bible or KJV) and label it; (c)
  paraphrase in the app's own words and label them "in our words". Same
  question applies to the `verse` references shown in big mode, which are
  references only (no text), so those are fine. Roadmap phase 8 already
  lists in-app scripture from a public-domain translation; this decision
  should be made with it. **Blocks release, not development.**

Length limits from the plan's roast: little questions ≤ 10 words (max 8),
little prayers ≤ 25 words (max 17), one activity each of one or two
sentences. Reading level: little text uses one-clause sentences; big text
assumes a reading parent.

Tone: no denominational claims; prayers address "Dear God" and close with
"Amen"; no mention of church, sacraments or specific doctrines. Nothing
frightening added beyond what the stories already show.
